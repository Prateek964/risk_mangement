PRACTICE QUESTION
THE QUESTION

A newly hired treasury risk analyst at a large bank has been assigned to the team responsible for managing the liquidity risk of the bank. The analyst is reviewing the tasks that will be required as part of this function. Which of the following is most likely part of the treasury risk analyst’s job duties?

Which task belongs to the treasury risk analyst?
❚❚ Pause & pick your answer 321
A✓✕
Building VaR models
B✓✕
Purchasing credit default swaps
C✓✕
Implementing asset-liability management
D✓✕
Estimating loss given default
CORRECT ANSWER
C✓✕
Implementing asset-liability management
ALM keeps funding liquidity in check
THE MISMATCH
Long-term loans funded by short-term deposits
ALM TOOLS
Gap analysis
Duration analysis
WHY NOT THE OTHERS?
A
Building VaR models
VaR models are built by model developers — and measure market risk.
B
Purchasing credit default swaps
CDS hedge counterparty risk — a form of credit risk.
D
Estimating loss given default
Loss given default quantifies credit risk, not liquidity.
KEY TAKEAWAY
1Treasury = funding liquidity risk
2Its main tool: asset-liability management
3VaR → market risk · CDS & LGD → credit risk

So, a newly hired treasury risk
analyst at a large bank joins
the team that manages the bank's
liquidity risk.
And the analyst is going through

job.
Which of these is most likely
part of the treasury risk analyst's
duties?
Option A.
Building VaR models.
Option B.
Purchasing credit default swaps.
Option C.
Implementing asset-liability management.
And option D.
Estimating loss given default.
Okay, pause the video and lock
in your answer.
And the answer is...
C!
Here's why.
Treasury looks after the bank's
funding liquidity risk,
and asset-liability management
is the main tool for that.
Think about it.
A bank often funds long-term loans
with short-term deposits.
That maturity mismatch hurts if
short-term rates rise faster than
long-term rates.
So the team uses gap analysis
and duration analysis to keep it
under control.
Now, why not the others?
A is wrong.
Building VaR models is a job
for model developers,
and VaR is mainly a market
risk tool.
B is wrong too.
Credit default swaps hedge counterparty
risk, which is a form of
credit risk.
And D?
Same story.
Estimating loss given default is
about quantifying credit risk,
not liquidity.
So, the key takeaway.
Treasury handles funding liquidity
risk, and its go-to tool is
asset-liability management.
VaR is market risk,
while credit default swaps and
loss given default are about credit
risk.
